# Kopi & Coins — Studio Brief

## Who we are
Kopi & Coins is a small Singapore content studio (fictitious). We make
60-second money stories for young working adults (22-35) on YouTube Shorts,
TikTok and Instagram Reels.

## The series
One family, one lesson per episode:
- **Rachel Tan, 29** — marketing executive; good at her job, not yet good
  with money.
- **Ah Ma, 74** — Rachel's grandmother; calm, dry humour; kept the family's
  savings in labelled envelopes inside a blue butter-cookie tin.
- **Daniel Lim, 30** — Rachel's colleague; spends every bonus before it lands.

## Brand
- Look: warm, hopeful, slice-of-life; soft natural light; Singapore HDB homes
  and cafés.
- Colours: kopi brown #6B4226, cream #FAF3E6, teal accent #2F7F7A.
- Font for on-screen text: a clean sans-serif, bold for the rule of the day.
- End card: the call to action, the Kopi & Coins logo, and the disclaimer.

## Rules we never break
1. General money education only. No product names, no recommendations, no
   promised or guaranteed returns.
2. Every episode ends with: "For general education only. Not financial advice."
3. Fictional people only. No real names, bank screens, NRIC numbers or
   phone numbers on screen.
4. Licensed or rights-cleared music only, with the licence recorded.
5. AI-generated content is disclosed when we upload.
6. Captions on every spoken line.
