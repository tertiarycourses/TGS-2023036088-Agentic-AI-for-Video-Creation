# Episode 1 — "The Rainy-Day Tin" (idea)

**Lesson:** build an emergency fund first — about 3 to 6 months of expenses
— before other goals.

**What happens:** Rachel's laptop dies the night before a client pitch. The
repair quote is $1,400; her bank app shows $312. Embarrassed, she video-calls
Ah Ma, who opens the old blue butter-cookie tin: inside, three labelled
envelopes — RAINY DAY, ANG PAO, HOLIDAY. "Every payday, rainy day first."
Rachel sets up three savings buckets with an automatic transfer on payday.
Six months later her phone screen cracks — and she pays for it calmly, with a
small blue tin on her desk.

**Platform:** 9:16 vertical, 60 seconds, captions on.
**Call to action:** "Start your rainy-day tin this payday."
