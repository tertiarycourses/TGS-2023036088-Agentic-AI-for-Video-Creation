# Script Review — Episode 1 (sample)

| Scene | Viewer should feel | Edit types | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Hook | Shock, recognition | Hard cuts; ECU inserts; text overlay | Fast cuts land the problem in under 3 s; the numbers on screen make it concrete |
| 2 Set-up | Embarrassment, warmth | J-cut (Ah Ma's "Wait ah" before we see her); MCU shot/reverse | Sound leading the picture makes the call feel real and intimate |
| 3 Turn | Surprise, curiosity | Cutaway (CU envelopes); match cut tin lid → banking app | The match cut links Ah Ma's habit to Rachel's modern version |
| 4 Choice | Determination | Montage of three paydays; text overlay | Compresses months into seconds; the rule becomes memorable |
| 5 Payoff | Relief, pride | Mirror the hook framing; held shot | The same framing with the opposite feeling shows the change |
| 6 End card | Clarity | Static graphic; 3+ s hold | Readable call to action and disclaimer |

**Stronger alternatives:** open scene 1 directly on the $1,400 quote (no
establishing shot); end scene 5 on the small tin, not on Rachel's face.

**Forecast:** AI identity drift on Rachel's glasses (shot 4-5) → repeat the
five traits and use the Element; garbled envelope labels → add text in the
editor; music over voice → duck 8 dB; missing disclaimer → end-card template.
