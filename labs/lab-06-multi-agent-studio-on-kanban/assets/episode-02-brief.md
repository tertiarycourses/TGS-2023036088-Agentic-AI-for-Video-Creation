# Episode 2 — "Daniel's Bonus" (brief)

**Lesson:** pay yourself first — when money arrives, set aside savings
before spending.

**Story:** Daniel gets his year-end bonus and has already planned a new
phone and a trip. Rachel, now calm with her rainy-day fund, shows him Ah Ma's
blue tin idea in a café. Daniel splits the bonus on the spot: rainy-day fund
top-up first, a goal for the trip, then the rest to enjoy. Final shot: Daniel
puts a small tin of his own on his desk.

**Format:** 60 s, 9:16, same characters and style as Episode 1, captions on.
**Call to action:** "When money comes in, pay yourself first."
**End card:** call to action + "For general education only. Not financial advice."
**Must avoid:** product names, percentages, promised returns.
