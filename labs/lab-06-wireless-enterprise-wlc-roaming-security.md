# Lab 06 - Enterprise Wireless, WLC, Roaming, and Security

## Objectives

- Explain enterprise wireless architecture.
- Identify AP and WLC roles.
- Review WLAN security.
- Understand roaming and RF basics.

## Steps

1. Draw an enterprise wireless architecture.
2. Identify AP, WLC, switch, RADIUS, DHCP, DNS, and client roles.
3. Explain CAPWAP at a high level.
4. Compare autonomous, lightweight, and cloud-managed APs.
5. Define an SSID and VLAN mapping.
6. Review WPA2, WPA3, PSK, and 802.1X.
7. Configure a WLAN profile if your lab tool supports it.
8. Review 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz channel planning.
9. Discuss interference, power levels, and coverage.
10. Explain Layer 2 and Layer 3 roaming.
11. Design a secure guest WLAN.
12. Document troubleshooting data to collect for wireless issues.

## Validation

- Wireless architecture is complete.
- WLAN security choices are explained.
- Roaming concepts are documented.
- Guest WLAN design separates guest and corporate traffic.

## Review Questions

1. What is the role of a WLC?
2. Why is 802.1X stronger than PSK for enterprise WLANs?
3. What can cause poor roaming?
4. Why should guest wireless be segmented?
