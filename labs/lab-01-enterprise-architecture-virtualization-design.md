# Lab 01 - Enterprise Architecture, Virtualization, and Design

## Objectives

- Compare enterprise design models.
- Identify campus, WAN, cloud, and data center roles.
- Explain virtualization and segmentation.
- Create an enterprise baseline topology.

## Steps

1. Draw a two-tier campus design.
2. Draw a three-tier campus design.
3. Label access, distribution, core, WAN edge, internet edge, data center, and cloud.
4. Explain where redundancy is required.
5. Identify control plane, data plane, and management plane functions.
6. Explain VRF as routing table segmentation.
7. Explain underlay and overlay at a high level.
8. Add a branch site and internet edge to your topology.
9. Create a device role table.
10. Create VLAN and addressing naming conventions.
11. Build the baseline topology in your lab tool.
12. Save the topology for later labs.

## Validation

- Architecture diagram shows clear network roles.
- Redundancy points are identified.
- Virtualization terms are explained correctly.
- Baseline topology is saved.

## Review Questions

1. Why do enterprise designs separate access, distribution, and core?
2. What is the purpose of a WAN edge?
3. How does VRF support segmentation?
4. What is the difference between overlay and underlay?
