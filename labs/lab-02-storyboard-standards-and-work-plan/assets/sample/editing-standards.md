# Editing Standards and Guidelines — Kopi & Coins (sample)

| # | Area | Rule | How we check | Standard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brand | End card shows logo, call to action and disclaimer for at least 3 s | Watch end card | Studio brand |
| 2 | Story | The problem is clear within 3 s; one message per episode | Phone viewing, sound off | Studio |
| 3 | Captions | Every spoken line captioned; 100% accurate; at most 2 lines; above the bottom 20% | Read every caption | WCAG 2.2 1.2.2; platform safe area |
| 4 | Audio | Mix at about -14 LUFS integrated; true peak no higher than -1 dBTP; music ducked under voice | Loudness meter | Platform normalisation; EBU R128 as reference |
| 5 | Rights | All music and images licensed or rights-cleared; licence logged | Licence log | Copyright Act 2021 |
| 6 | Personal data | No real names, ID numbers, phone numbers or account screens | Frame check | PDPA |
| 7 | Money messaging | General education only; no products, no guaranteed returns; disclaimer "For general education only. Not financial advice." | Script and VO check | Financial Advisers Act; SCAP (ASAS) |
| 8 | AI labelling | AI-content disclosure switched on at upload | Upload plan | Platform policies |
| 9 | Platform | 1080x1920, 9:16, constant 30 fps, H.264 MP4, 55-60 s | File info | Platform specifications |
